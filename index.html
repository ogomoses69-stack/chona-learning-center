OUR STORY

Growing learners.
Shaping futures.

From its humble beginning, Chona And Learning Center has continued to grow as an institution committed to nurturing learners from the early years through secondary education.

Our approach goes beyond traditional classroom learning. Through academics, technology, practical activities, sports, creativity, discipline and leadership development, students are encouraged to discover their abilities and prepare for the future.

OUR VISION

“To nurture future leaders who are academically sound, technologically skilled and able to excel dynamically in the world.”
